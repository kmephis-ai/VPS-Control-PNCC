# License Decision Required

This repository is public for engineering transparency and collaboration, but a project-wide open-source license is intentionally **not selected yet**.

Before choosing a license, the migration must establish:
- provenance of legacy V6/V7 source;
- whether any third-party snippets were incorporated;
- provenance/licenses of bundled PuTTY/Proxifier-related binaries or other dependencies;
- future plugin dependency/license boundaries;
- whether GPL/AGPL or other reciprocal components are linked, bundled or merely integrated externally.

Until that review is complete, public visibility must not be interpreted as permission to reuse the source beyond rights already provided by applicable law or component licenses.
